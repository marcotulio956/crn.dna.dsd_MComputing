	2*a_{ab} -fast-> a_{ab}

example:
a->b

2 inverter or absence indicator  with fast reactions:
	fuel -slow-> a_{ab}
	a + a_{ab} -fast-> a
	2*a_{ab} -fast-> a_{ab}

	a_{ab} -slow-> a'_{ab}
	a+a'_{ab} -fast-> a
	2*a'_{ab} -fast-> a'_{ab}

3 increment and decrement operations:
	let b be the start signal and x the quantity to operate.

	3.1 the system waits for the start signal g to be some nonzero quantity
	3.2. it transfers the quantity of x to a temporary type x'
	3.3. it sets g to zero
	3.4. it transfers all but one molecule of x' back to x
	3.5.1 for decrement, it removes the last molecule x'
	3.5.2 for increment, it removes the last molecule of x' and adds to two molecules to x

	in terms of equations:

	x + g -slow-> x' + g, transfer molecules of x to molecules of x' as the control g is inserted
	g + x_{ab} -slow-> zero, the control g is set to zero only after the absence of x(denoted by x_{ab}) which indicates all molecules of x have been transfered to x'.
	g'_{ab}  + 2x' -fast-> x + x' + x^{rx}, g'_{ab} is a secondary absence indicator by using fast reactions.
	x^{rx} -slow-> zero, x^{rx} indicate that we are currently transferring molecules of x' back to x.
	x^{rx}_{ab} + x' + g'_{ab} -slow-> zero, decrement
	x^{rx}_{ab} + x' + g'_{ab} -slow-> 2x, increment
	
	with this module a small modification on the 3rd eq is possible to implement a division by 2:
	g'_{ab} + 2x' -fast-> x + x^{rx}

4 copier
	g + a -slow-> g + a', in the presence of g, a reaction transfersthe quantity of a to a'
	g + a_{ab} -slow-> zero, after all molecules of a have been transferred to a', molecules of g are removed
	g_{ab} + a' -slow-> a + b, removing g  ensures a is copied exactly once, after g has been removed, transfer the quantity of a' back to a and also creates this same quantity of b
